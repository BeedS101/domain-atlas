Why We Keep a Reading Nook

A short post about the corner of the Lobby by the bookcase.

Most of what happens in the Lobby is people passing through — the loot crates get all the attention. But the bookcase corner is where the actual reading happens, and this post is about that corner specifically, not the Lobby as a whole.

A blog post is exactly the kind of page SPEC.md §3.5 was written for: ordinary text content that happens to be about one small place inside a much bigger space, with no reason to make visitors find that place themselves.

This page spawns you at the nook, not at the Lobby's front door

The home page and every other Lobby link on this site drop a visitor at the Lobby's ordinary entry point, camera and all. This page names lobby:reading-nook, a specific named anchor inside that same Lobby world (SPEC.md §3.5) — so a Domain Atlas-aware browser extension entering from here starts the camera right at the reading nook instead, with a 📍 marker on the Enter button to show it. Enter from the home page afterward and compare the starting view.
