This page points at one exact spot, not the domain's front door

Every other page on this site (the home page, the other demos below) drops a visitor at Example Plaza's own default entry point. This page is different — it names plaza:compass-stall, a specific named anchor inside that same Plaza world (SPEC.md §3.5), so a Domain Atlas-aware browser extension entering from here lands right at the Compass Stall instead, with a small 📍 marker on the Enter button and in the scene itself to show it.
