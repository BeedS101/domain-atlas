A two-minute, no-installation demo of Domain Atlas's credential layer.

Click below and this page issues you two real, cryptographically signed credentials — a coupon you can hand off to someone else, and a badge that's permanently yours. Send the coupon on, or give up either one yourself once you're done with it — both keys involved are generated right here in your browser and never leave it except as a signature; every action below is a genuine call to this domain's own server, not a mockup.

What this looks like for a real business

Swap "coupon" for a loyalty voucher a till can check without ever calling your server, and "badge" for a staff pass, a season ticket, or a membership card handed in when someone leaves. Same three actions either way: issue it straight into someone's wallet, let them move it on or redeem it themselves, and let anyone — a partner, a scanner, an auditor — confirm it's real without ever asking you.
