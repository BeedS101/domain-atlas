      Open a coin on the ledger

      A registered coin is only a record. Opening it creates its ledger on this domain through spend34.php: entry 0 credits the treasury. A coin that is already open is refused, so nothing is opened twice. A multi authority coin opens only when every partner domain has signed its genesis; if one does not answer, press the button again later and only the missing ones are asked. After that the coin is in the scanner, and a wallet that adds this domain sees what it holds.
