Set up your wallet

Password identity
One password protects your identity on this device. You'll enter it once per browser session — Domain Atlas doesn't ask again until you fully close and reopen the browser. There is no password reset. If you lose your password and your seed phrase together, your identity and everything owned by it cannot be recovered — not by this wallet, not by us, not by anyone.
Passkey identity
Uses a hardware-backed passkey instead — Windows Hello, Touch ID, or a security key. No password to remember or lose, but the key never leaves the device/authenticator: it can't be exported or backed up to a file the way the password identity can.
